How Predictions Work

Prediction sources

  • If a locked pregame snapshot exists after the cutoff window, the app reuses that snapshot so the displayed pick does not drift after kickoff.
  • If no locked snapshot exists, the app tries recent team history, then full history, then market consensus from Sportsbet h2h odds.
  • If neither usable history nor odds are available, a deterministic fallback prediction is shown, but confidence is low.

Main prediction fields

Prediction label

The highest-probability outcome: home team, away team, or draw.

Win chance

The displayed lean percentage. In market mode, the strongest outcome is clamped into a narrow display band before being shown.

Edge

The gap between the top and runner-up probabilities. Larger gaps produce stronger visual tiers and a higher confidence score.

Confidence

Derived from edge size, probability agreement, and sample size. Fallback predictions are treated more conservatively.

Stats on each card

  • History win uses prior outcomes for the chosen side after conservative weighting and sample-size smoothing.
  • Model win uses the model-estimated probability after additional tightening.
  • Odds implied is computed from decimal odds as 1 / odds.
  • Home win and Away win show venue-specific win rates from historical results.
  • Home score, Away score, and Both score estimate scoring likelihood from recent form.
  • Venue edge compares home venue win rate against away venue performance.

How the visuals are used

  • The color tiers are display-only: red means weak, orange means moderate, green means strong, and gold means strongest.
  • Win-chance badges use separate thresholds from the stat tiers, so a card can look strong in one dimension and average in another.
  • The win-rate filter hides predictions below the minimum visible threshold, which is currently 50%.
  • Top-bet suggestions and result cards reuse the same prediction object, so the same calculation drives all views.

These are conservative heuristics, not guarantees. The app is designed to compare market signals and recent form, then present the result with clear confidence and history context.
